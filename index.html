HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

Guide the fallen angel along the cloud path, destroy the light that stands in your way, then defeat the guardian angel in the final boss fight.

PC / LAPTOP CONTROLS

  • W or ↑ : jump to the upper cloud
  • S or ↓ : drop to the lower cloud
  • D : attack
  • E : shield (final boss only)

MOBILE CONTROLS

Use the ▲ button to jump, ▼ to drop, ATTACK to shoot, and SHIELD (it only appears in the final boss fight).

ABOUT

Fallen Angel is a story of rebellion against God's command. In many legends, a fallen angel is a heavenly being who refused to bow to the divine order and was cast out of the sky. This game is a fictional, symbolic interpretation, not a statement of belief.

FALLEN ANGEL

You. The dark wings and dress stand for purity cast aside, the red eyes for rage and resolve, and the cracked red halo for heavenly honor now stained. The blonde hair is a last trace of heaven's light. It keeps moving forward because there is no way back.

LIGHT

The divine command and order hunting the rebel. The light that once protected becomes a punishment: blinding, cold, and offering no room for questions.

FIRE

The fire of rebellion: free will, courage, and the desire to choose your own path. Fire burns and illuminates at once, so every flame strengthens the rebel's attack.

GUARDIAN ANGEL (FINAL BOSS)

The angel who stayed obedient: perfect loyalty, and a shadow of who you used to be. Its orbs of light are the final judgment. To fight it is to cut the last tie with heaven.
